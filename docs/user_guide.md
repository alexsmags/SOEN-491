# User Guide: AI-Based Image Caption Generator

## Overview
The **AI-Based Image Caption Generator** is a web app that lets you upload images, automatically generate captions using AI, customize them, and share your results on social media.

This guide explains how to use the app step by step.

---

## 3. Generating Captions

Once your image is uploaded, you can generate a caption with the built-in AI parameters to control the style and output.  

---

### 3.1 Parameters Overview

#### Style
- **Tone** – Choose the tone of your caption (e.g: Casual, Formal, Humorous, Professional).  
- **Voice** – Set how the caption “sounds” (e.g: Neutral, I, We).  
- **Length** – Control how long the caption will be (Short, Medium, or Long).  
- **Keywords** – Add up to 8 keywords to shape how the caption is written.  

---

#### Hashtags
- Add up to **8 hashtags**
- Choose whether to:
  - **Append hashtags** automatically.  
  - Set **Hashtag placement** (Start or End or Middle of caption).  


---

#### Mentions & Location
- Enable **@mentions** to tag users or brands.  
- Optionally include a **location** (e.g: “Montreal, QC”).  
- Choose where mentions and locations appear (Start or End or Middle).  


---

#### Emojis
- Allow the AI to add **relevant emojis** automatically.  
- Set how many emojis to include (e.g: 1–3).  
- Choose where emojis appear (Start or End of caption).  

---

### 3.2 Generating the Caption
- After setting all parameters, click **Generate Caption**.  
- The AI will analyze your image and create a caption based on your selected style and preferences.  
- You’ll see the result instantly, ready for editing or saving.

## 7. Tips for Best Results
- Use **clear, high-quality images** with visible subjects.  
- Experiment with different **caption tones** to find the best one. 
- Keep captions short and natural for social media.
- Add more keywords if AI-Wrapper is not depicting well the image.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting
| Issue | Possible Cause | Solution |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Upload fails | File too large or unsupported format | Resize or convert the image (JPG/PNG). |
| Caption not generated | Network or AI service delay (sometimes not cached) | Wait a few seconds and retry. |
| Sharing doesn’t work | Social login expired | Reconnect your account and try again. |
